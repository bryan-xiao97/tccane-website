# TCCA NE Website

The website for the **Tzu Chi Collegiate Association, Northeast Region (TCCA NE)**.

It exists to do two things:

- **Share information** about TCCA NE — who we are, our missions, the chapters, and how student members get involved.
- **Collect general interest** — a short intake form for prospective members and volunteers to reach out and join the community.

## What's on the site

- **About** — an introduction to TCCA NE and the values behind it.
- **Why join** — what students gain from being part of the community.
- **Four missions** — the core areas of Tzu Chi's work the association is built around.
- **Life in the chapters** — a look at what members actually do day to day.
- **Chapters** — the campuses and groups that make up the Northeast Region.
- **Resources** — links and materials for members and newcomers.
- **Get involved** — the interest form for prospective members and volunteers.
